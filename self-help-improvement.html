Unspoken Room

Available now

Unspoken Room is a standalone Mac app for private local AI conversations. Version 1.0 includes Qwen3 4B, Parakeet speech recognition, 20 Kokoro voices, typed chat, continuous read-along text, a Hugging Face GGUF library, and local GGUF import.

Unspoken Room is available now for $29.99 in the U.S. Mac App Store as a one-time purchase.

View on the Mac App Store Visit unspokenroom.app

Threshold Lab

Waiting for Review

Threshold Lab is an audio laboratory for designing, understanding, and exporting original guided listening sessions. Version 1.0 has been submitted to App Store Connect and is waiting for App Review. It is not yet approved or available to download.
