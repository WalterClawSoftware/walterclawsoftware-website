# Walter Claw Software Homepage Directions

These three standalone prototypes explore the same approved information architecture without changing the production homepage:

- two clearly separated paths: free utility apps, or paid apps in development for self-help, improvement, and understanding;
- Simple Voice Reader leads the six free utilities and includes its iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Windows coverage;
- the remaining free utilities are ClipScript Desktop, Transcript Rescue, Repro Pack, BannerSafe, and App Icon Creator Desktop;
- the paid-app path names Unspoken Room and Threshold Lab;
- no release dates or unverified product features are claimed.

## Options

1. [Option A: Two Clear Doors](option-a-two-clear-doors.html) — the clearest equal-footing path choice, with the free path given more visual weight.
2. [Option B: Guided Choice](option-b-guided-choice.html) — a warmer editorial direction built around the question, "What brings you here?"
3. [Option C: Simple Voice Reader Beacon](option-c-simple-voice-reader-beacon.html) — the most product-forward direction, opening with Simple Voice Reader before the two-path choice.

These are decision artifacts only. The production `index.html` and shared `styles.css` remain unchanged until one direction is selected.
